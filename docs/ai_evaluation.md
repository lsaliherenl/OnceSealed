## Labels

Annotators: Salih Eren Çavuşoğlu (human) and Claude Opus 5.5 (ai), labelling independently and blind to every evaluated model's answers. Both answered yes/no on 173 of 177 questions; raw agreement 95.4%, **Cohen's kappa 0.90**; 4 questions marked unclear by at least one annotator.

One annotator is an AI model (Claude), so this kappa is human-AI agreement, not agreement between two people. Claude is not one of the evaluated models.

Gold labels: adjudicated (`data/eval/gold.json`).
Each of the 12 disagreements was settled by the human annotator against the excerpt (human reading kept 7, AI reading kept 5); the decisions and the 7 guide rules they produced are in `data/eval/adjudication.json`.

Gold set: 177 questions, 58 yes (32.8%).
